WorthKnowing Revenue Pilot

A private-owner publishing tool that uses Google OAuth to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

The tool requests YouTube upload and read-only access solely to verify the authorized channel, avoid duplicate uploads, publish approved videos, and confirm publication status. It is not offered to third parties.

Data use

Google user data is used only to operate the owner's YouTube channel. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
